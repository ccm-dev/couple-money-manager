About v1.60 beta 30

Couple Money Manager is a single-file app for couples that works offline. Your data lives on this device and cloud sync is opt-in.
Help & tips
  1. Add: tap ＋, pick a category chip, then Save. Your last category is remembered.
  2. Edit: tap a transaction. Select several: long-press one.
  3. Refunds: tap “Refund” next to the amount to record a reduction in expenses.
  4. Split a bill, refund or income: use the “Split this bill” box on the form, and type each share as a positive amount. For a bill, the other person's share is added to the “X owes Y” ledger. For a refund, it is taken off. For income received into your account, their share is owed back to them. Settle it from Between Us.
  5. Credit cards: set a statement day and payment-due days in Accounts → the account → Edit.
  6. Budgets: Settings → App Preference → Budgets. Set fixed and variable amounts per category, as a couple budget or a personal one.
  7. Saving goals: Settings → App Preference → Saving goals. Link a goal to a savings account, or add money to it by hand.
  8. Reports: every report has Week / Month / Year and Couple / individual options.
  9. Split bills: open any share, then choose This share only or Whole bill to edit. Delete asks whether to remove one share or the whole bill.
  10. Money assistant: tap Ask at the top. Answers come from your data on this device; you can optionally connect OpenAI or Gemini with your own key.
  11. Privacy: Settings → Privacy & Security. Set a PIN, choose what the Privacy Screen hides and how the device is protected. Mark any transaction Private under More options.
  12. Household sync: sign in with Google or email in Settings → Modes, then invite your partner by email. You both see the same data, updated live.
  13. Hide from your partner: mark a transaction Private, or hide an account balance, in Settings → Modes.
  14. Statements: Settings → Data & Sync → Import a bank / card statement. Review every line before it is added.
  15. Calculator: tap the calculator icon inside an amount box.
  16. Getting started: follow the checklist on the Dashboard. Each “Show me” guides you step by step while you do it yourself.
  17. Sample data: Settings → Your Profile → Add or Remove sample transactions.
  18. Back: the phone Back button or Esc closes the top screen.
