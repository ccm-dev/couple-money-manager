About v1.60 beta 130

Eucie is a private money app for individuals and couples that works offline. Your data lives on this device and cloud sync is opt-in.
Help & tips
  1. Add: tap ＋, pick a category chip, then Save. Your last category is remembered.
  2. Edit: tap a transaction. Select several: long-press one.
  3. Signed amounts: a positive Expense or Income is normal. Type a negative amount to reduce expenses or income.
  4. Split a bill, refund or income: tap the Split option on the form, choose Equal or Custom shares, and use the keypad to edit a share. The balance line and the “owes” preview update as you type. For a bill, the other person's share is added to the “X owes Y” ledger. For a refund, it is taken off. For income received into your account, their share is owed back to them. Settle it from Between Us.
  5. Credit cards: set a statement day and payment-due days in Accounts → the account → Edit.
  6. Budgets: Settings → App Preference → Budgets. Set fixed and variable amounts per category, as a couple budget or a personal one.
  7. Saving goals: Settings → App Preference → Saving goals. Link a goal to a savings account, or add money to it by hand.
  8. Reports: every report has Week / Month / Year and Couple / individual options.
  9. Split bills: a split bill is one grouped row — tap it to open whole-bill Details. Edit and Copy act on the whole bill (Copy keeps the split, sets today's date and omits Repeat). Delete removes every share and reverses the Between Us entry, with Undo.
  10. Money assistant: tap Ask at the top. Answers come from your data on this device; you can optionally connect OpenAI or Gemini with your own key.
  11. Privacy: Settings → Privacy & Security. Set a PIN, choose what the Privacy Screen hides and how the device is protected. Mark any transaction Private under More options.
  12. Household sync: sign in with Google or email in Settings → Modes, then invite your partner with a link you copy or share. You both see the same data, updated live.
  13. Hide from your partner: mark a transaction Private, or hide an account balance, in Settings → Modes.
  14. Statements: Settings → Data & Sync → Import a bank / card statement. Review every line before it is added.
  15. Calculator: tap the calculator icon inside an amount box.
  16. Getting started: follow the first steps at the top of Home. Each “Show me” guides you step by step while you do it yourself.
  17. Sample data: Settings → Data & Sync → Sample data → Try with sample data or Remove sample data. It stays on this phone.
  18. Back: the phone Back button or Esc closes the top screen.
