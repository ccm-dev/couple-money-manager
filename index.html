Advanced: sync through a Drive / OneDrive file
  1. Android + Chrome: tap “Link cloud file” and pick a file in your Drive / OneDrive folder. Changes are saved to it automatically.
  2. iPhone: download a backup file and save it to Files → Drive / OneDrive. Bring it back with Import files.
  3. Sharing: both of you link the same file in a shared folder. The last save wins.
Privacy

By default, nothing leaves this device. If you turn on Google cloud sync, your email and data are stored in Google Cloud (Firebase, asia-southeast1), encrypted in transit and at rest, and only your account can read them. “Delete cloud data” removes the cloud copy. Anonymous app-open analytics never include transactions.

Import & Export

Imports are merged with your data. Nothing is overwritten and duplicates are skipped.

About v1.52

Couple Money Manager is a single-file app for couples that works offline. Your data lives on this device and cloud sync is opt-in.
Help & tips
  1. Add: tap ＋, pick a category chip, then Save. Your last category is remembered.
  2. Edit: tap a transaction. Select several: long-press one.
  3. Refunds: tap “Refund” next to the amount to record a reduction in expenses.
  4. Split a bill, refund or income: use the “Split this bill” box on the form, and type each share as a positive amount. For a bill, the other person's share is added to the “X owes Y” ledger. For a refund, it is taken off. For income received into your account, their share is owed back to them. Settle it from Who owes whom.
  5. Credit cards: set a statement day and payment-due days in Settings → Accounts.
  6. Budgets: Settings → Budgets. Set fixed and variable amounts per category, as a couple budget or a personal one.
  7. Saving goals: Settings → Saving goals. Link a goal to a savings account, or add money to it by hand.
  8. Reports: every report has Week / Month / Year and Couple / individual options.
  9. Collaborate: Settings → Collaborators → Invite. Choose which accounts they may manage, send the invite file, and import their changes file when they send it back.
  10. Split bills: open any share, then choose This share only or Whole bill to edit. Delete asks whether to remove one share or the whole bill.
  11. Money assistant: tap Ask at the top. Answers come from your data on this device; you can optionally connect OpenAI or Gemini with your own key.
  12. Privacy: Settings → Privacy. Set a PIN, hide amounts, choose whether notes are shared. Mark any transaction Private under More options.
  13. Cloud: sign in with Google or Microsoft in Settings → Data & Sync. Changes then sync automatically.
  14. Statements: Settings → Import bank / card statement. Review every line before it is added.
  15. Calculator: tap the calculator icon inside an amount box.
  16. Getting started: follow the checklist on the Overview. Each “Show me” guides you step by step while you do it yourself.
  17. Sample data: Settings → Your account → Add or Remove sample transactions.
  18. Back: the phone Back button or Esc closes the top screen.
